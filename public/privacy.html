Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us through the brief or the say-hello form: your email address, the project details you enter, and your message. There is no account system and no newsletter.

How it is processed

Form submissions are delivered to us through Formspark, a third-party form processor (see Formspark’s privacy policy). We use what you send solely to read and respond to your inquiry.

Spam protection

The contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA v2 to tell people apart from bots (see Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service). reCAPTCHA loads only when you open the contact form, not on page load.

Cookies and analytics

This site currently sets no analytics or marketing cookies and runs no analytics. If we introduce analytics in the future, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where required.

Fonts and embeds

Fonts are self-hosted on this site. No external font service is contacted when you visit.

Retention and your rights

We keep inquiries for as long as we need them to respond and follow up. To ask what we hold about you, or to request that we delete it, write to info@creativecodelogic.com.

Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it last changed.
